# Gather

Gather is an all-in-one, mobile event planning app designed for event coordinators who want to take planning more seriously. Whether organizing a small gathering, a celebration, a professional event, or a large-scale experience, Gather brings the details together in one place.

With **Gala**, your AI-powered elephant companion, you get support throughout the planning process - from organizing tasks and managing guests to creating menus and keeping everything on schedule.

## Features

- **Event Management:** Create, organize, and manage events of all kinds.
- **AI Companion - Gala:** Get assistance with planning, ideas, and event coordination.
- **Guest Management:** Organize guest lists, invitations, and RSVPs.
- **Tasks & Scheduling:** Track responsibilities, deadlines, and event timelines.
- **Budget Planning:** Organize expenses and keep budgets in check.
- **Food & Menu Planning:** Coordinate food, drinks, and menu details.
- **Settings:** Manage your account and app preferences.
